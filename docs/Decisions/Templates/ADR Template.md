# NNNN. Title (short, imperative — "Use X for Y", not "Discussion of X")

## Status

proposed | accepted | superseded by [[Decisions/NNNN-title|ADR NNNN]]

## Context

What forces are at play — technical, team, timeline, capstone constraints? State the problem, not the solution.

## Decision

What we're doing, stated plainly.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result. Include real tradeoffs, not just upside — if there were no downsides it wouldn't need a decision record.
